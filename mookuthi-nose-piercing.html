Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before your piercing
01

Do you pierce with a needle or a gun?

We use sterile, single-use needles, and every piercing is done on-site at the studio.

02

Which side should I get my nose pierced on?

It is a personal choice. Traditionally, many women wear the mookuthi on the left, but there is no wrong side. Our team can help you decide in front of the mirror.

03

Does a nose piercing hurt?

Most people feel a quick pinch that passes in seconds. Our experts talk you through each step, so you know what to expect.

04

How do I care for a new nose piercing?

Clean it twice a day with a saline solution, avoid touching or twisting the pin, and keep makeup and creams away from the area.

05

How long does a nose piercing take to heal?

A nostril piercing usually takes two to four months to heal on the surface and can take up to six months to settle fully. Keep your first mookuthi in until then.

06

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. Booking through the form above guarantees you a slot at the time you want.
